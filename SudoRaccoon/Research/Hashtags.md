# Hashtags

Built by `scripts/hashtags.py` from competitor captions ([[Competitors]]). How to use it: [[Caption and hashtags]].
**Lift** = median of (video views ÷ that account's median views) for videos using the tag: 1.0× is normal for the account, above 1 beats it. Tags used fewer than 2 times are left out. Read lift together with **uses**; a 3× tag seen on 2 videos is a hint, not proof.
